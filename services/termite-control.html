Professional Methodology

How Our DRILL-FILL-SEAL Termite Barrier Works

Subterranean termites (commonly called Chedalu in Telugu) originate from deep inside the soil beneath your building foundation. They build mud tubes along skirting boards, walls, and door frames to reach wood, cellulose paper, and drywall. Traditional superficial spraying only kills surface foragers while leaving the queen colony unharmed.

Green Pest Management Solution deploys the industry-standard DRILL-FILL-SEAL protocol with Central Insecticides Board (CIB) approved non-repellent termiticides (Imidacloprid / Fipronil):

  1. Precision Wall & Skirting Drilling: Technicians drill 12mm holes at a 45-degree angle where the wall meets the floor slab, spaced every 1 foot along internal perimeter walls and wooden fixtures.
  2. High-Pressure Chemical Injection: Government-approved odorless chemical emulsion is pumped into each hole at controlled pressure to form a continuous barrier inside the masonry.
  3. Colony Transfer Eradication: The non-repellent active agent allows foraging termites to pass through without alarm. They transfer the chemical to the nest via grooming, causing total colony elimination within 7 to 10 days.
  4. Color-Matched Cosmetic Sealing: Each drilled hole is neatly sealed with white cement and pigment to preserve floor and wall aesthetics.

Frequently Asked Questions

Termite Control FAQs

Answers to common questions about anti-termite treatment in Hyderabad homes.

Do we need to vacate our home during the treatment?

No. We use premium low-odor or odorless CIB-approved chemicals (like Imidacloprid). You do not need to vacate the house, although we recommend keeping small children and pets out of the immediate room while drilling is underway.

Will the drilling damage our tiles or wooden floor?

Our technicians use specialized masonry drill bits to drill small 12mm holes right at the junction of the wall skirting and floor. Once chemical injection is finished, every hole is neatly filled with matching white cement or silicon sealant so it is virtually invisible.

How soon will the termites die?

Active termites in wood frames are knocked down within 24 to 48 hours. Because of the transfer effect, the subterranean colony (including the reproductive queen) collapses within 7 to 14 days.

How does the 5-year warranty work?

You receive a signed warranty bill on service day. If you notice any mud tubes or termite emergence during 5 years, just call or WhatsApp our helpline. A senior technician will visit and conduct complimentary re-treatment.
