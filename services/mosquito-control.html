Dual-Barrier Protection

Advanced Adulticide Thermal Fogging & Residual Misting

Hyderabad experiences seasonal surges in viral fevers, particularly Dengue (spread by daytime-biting Aedes aegypti mosquitoes) and Malaria (spread by evening-active Anopheles). With lush landscaping, basement parkings, and stormwater drains in areas like Hafeezpet, Kondapur, and Gachibowli, adult mosquito swarms settle in bushes and dark alcoves during daylight hours.

Green Pest Management provides a comprehensive dual-barrier treatment designed for residential estates, gated societies, and corporate campuses:

  1. Outdoor Thermal Pulse-Jet Fogging: Industrial pulse-jet thermal machines generate dense, fine smoke droplets containing CIB-approved synthetic pyrethroids. The thermal fog penetrates thick garden foliage, perimeter hedges, basement parking lots, and drainage lines, delivering an immediate knock-down of flying swarms.
  2. Indoor Residual Cold Misting: High-precision ultra-low volume (ULV) cold misting applies an odorless, non-staining micro-layer of Deltamethrin to exterior walls, balcony railings, door frames, and curtain folds where resting mosquitoes land, providing up to 4 weeks of residual repellency.
  3. Pre-Event & Society AMC Programs: Customized schedules for evening lawn parties, weddings, outdoor banquets, and recurring weekly/bi-weekly fogging visits for gated apartment associations.

Safe & Certified Insecticide Formulations

All fogging solutions utilize WHO-prequalified, government-certified formulations mixed with deodorized carrier bases, ensuring minimal irritation to residents while delivering lethal efficacy against adult vectors.

Frequently Asked Questions

Mosquito Fogging FAQs

Clear answers about timing, safety, and effectiveness of thermal fogging.

What is the best time of day to conduct outdoor thermal fogging?

Thermal fogging is most effective during early morning (6:00 AM – 8:00 AM) or dusk (5:30 PM – 7:00 PM). During these cooler hours, thermal inversion keeps the smoke close to the ground where mosquitoes are actively flying and seeking shelter.

Is the thermal fog smoke toxic to plants or pets?

No. The formulations are strictly CIB-approved for public health pest management. They leave zero harmful residues on ornamental garden plants, lawns, or trees. We advise keeping pets indoors while the fog is being generated for 15 minutes until it clears.

How long does one thermal fogging session remain effective?

Thermal smoke kills adult mosquitoes present at that time. When combined with our residual wall spray, adult mosquitoes are repelled for up to 2 to 3 weeks. For long-term control, combining fogging with anti-larval treatment is recommended.

Can we book single-day fogging for an outdoor party or wedding?

Yes! We regularly provide pre-event fogging for evening lawn parties, marriage receptions, and family gatherings. We conduct the fogging 2 to 3 hours prior to guest arrival so the grounds are mosquito-free without any lingering smoke.
