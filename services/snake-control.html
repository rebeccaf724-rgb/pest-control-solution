Prevention around your property

Keeping Snakes Away From Your Home

Cobras, rat snakes and kraits are found across Hyderabad's outskirts and newer gated communities, especially near open plots, lakes and farmland. Snakes are protected wildlife in India, so the safest long-term answer is to keep them out, not to kill them.

Our snake control covers the three things that bring snakes in:

  1. Property inspection: We walk the boundary, garden, drains, sumps, parking and store rooms to find where snakes can enter and shelter.
  2. Repellent treatment: A snake-repellent treatment is applied along compound walls, gates, drain openings and garden edges.
  3. Rats and entry points: Rats and frogs that attract snakes are controlled, and we show you the gaps under gates, drain grills and pipe holes to close.

If a snake is already inside

Keep everyone, including pets, away from it, close the door to that room if you can do so safely, and call us. We arrange removal through trained snake handlers, who release snakes away from homes as wildlife rules require.

Frequently Asked Questions

Snake Control FAQs

Answers to common questions before you book.

Do you kill snakes?

No. Snakes are protected wildlife in India. Our treatment keeps them away, and when a snake must be removed we arrange trained handlers who release it away from homes.

What attracts snakes to a house?

Mainly rats and frogs, plus shelter in debris, stacked material, dense plants and open drains. Removing these is a big part of keeping snakes out.

What should I do if I see a snake at home?

Stay calm, keep people and pets away, do not try to catch or hit it, close the room door if it is safe, and call us or a local snake rescue line.

Is the repellent safe for my pets?

It is applied along the boundary and drains, not inside living areas. We tell you how long to keep pets away from treated edges.
