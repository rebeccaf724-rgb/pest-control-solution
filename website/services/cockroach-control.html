Domino Cascade Technology

How Advanced Gel Baiting Destroys Hidden Cockroach Nests

German cockroaches (the tiny brown roaches clustering inside kitchen hinges, microwave panels, and under sinks) are notorious for reproducing at alarming speeds. Traditional aerosol sprays only scatter them deeper into hollow tiles and false ceilings.

Green Pest Management Solution uses an advanced Dual-Action Gel Baiting & Drainage Sanitization protocol:

  1. Precision Micro-Dot Gel Placement: Technicians place tiny dots of food-grade attraction gel bait along cabinet hinges, drawer glides, behind refrigerators, under sink traps, and inside electrical switchboard cavities.
  2. Cascade Domino Ingestion: Roaches ingest the high-potency fipronil bait and return to their hiding nests. When they die, other roaches consume their feces and carcasses, spreading the active agent to the entire colony.
  3. Drainage & Manhole Crack Spray: Bathroom drains, kitchen gullies, and plumbing shafts are treated with targeted residual spray to halt large American flying roaches from entering from municipal sewer lines.

Frequently Asked Questions

Cockroach Control FAQs

Everything you need to know about odorless gel treatment for your kitchen.

Do I really not need to empty my kitchen cabinets?

Yes, really! Unlike old-fashioned pungent chemical sprays, our gel bait is applied in tiny, targeted drops on door hinges, corner joints, and under shelves. Your utensils, crockery, and food supplies can stay right where they are.

Is the gel bait safe around toddlers and pets?

Yes. The gel bait is applied in concealed crevices and hinges that are completely inaccessible to children and pets. It emits zero toxic vapors or fumes.

How quickly will we see results?

You will notice dead roaches within 12 to 24 hours. Because the poisoned roaches pass the bait to the rest of the nest, total eradication of the colony occurs within 3 to 5 days.

What happens if I see a cockroach after 3 months?

You have a 6-month warranty. Just send a quick WhatsApp message or call our number. Our technician will come to your house and apply fresh gel bait free of charge.
