Breeding sites and resting surfaces

Fly Control That Lasts Beyond the Visit

A single fly can lay hundreds of eggs in a garbage bin or wet drain, and they hatch within a day in Hyderabad's heat. Spraying the flies you can see brings quick relief, but they return unless the breeding sites are treated too.

Our treatment works in three parts:

  1. Inspection: We find where flies breed and rest: garbage areas, drains, wet waste, animal sheds, ceilings, lights and window frames.
  2. Breeding site treatment: Garbage points, drains and waste areas are treated to stop larvae from growing into adult flies.
  3. Bait and residual spray: Fly bait is placed and a residual spray is applied to the surfaces flies rest on, away from food.

Frequently Asked Questions

Fly Control FAQs

Answers to common questions before you book.

Why do flies come back after spraying?

Because new flies keep hatching from garbage, drains and wet waste. Treating those breeding sites is what keeps numbers down.

Is fly treatment safe in a restaurant kitchen?

Yes. Bait and spray are kept away from food, utensils and serving areas, and we tell your staff when the kitchen can be used again.

Do you offer regular fly control for businesses?

Yes. Restaurants, hotels and food units can take a monthly or quarterly plan with a service record for inspections.

How can I reduce flies at home?

Keep bins closed and emptied daily, clean drains and sink traps, and do not leave wet waste or pet food out. We give you a short checklist after treatment.
