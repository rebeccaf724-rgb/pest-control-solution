Scientific Larval Source Management

Why Anti-Larval Treatment is 90% More Effective than Fogging Alone

A single female mosquito deposits between 100 and 300 eggs every few days in standing water. Within 48 hours, these eggs hatch into mobile larvae (commonly called wrigglers), which develop into pupae and take flight as disease-carrying adults in just 7 to 10 days.

In Hyderabad's rapidly developing corridors—such as Hafeezpet, Kondapur, Madhapur, Tellapur, and Nanakramguda—construction water curing tanks, elevator sump pits, unsealed rainwater harvesting wells, and stormwater gullies become breeding grounds that produce millions of mosquitoes every month.

Our 3-Step Anti-Larval Protocol

  1. Water Source Mapping & Dipper Audit: Certified inspectors map every collection point, testing water samples with scientific dippers to determine the species, density, and larval development stage.
  2. Targeted Biological & IGR Application: We apply Central Insecticides Board (CIB) and WHO-prequalified larvicides:
    • BTI (Bacillus thuringiensis israelensis): A natural biological bacterium that is lethal specifically to mosquito and midge larvae upon ingestion, while harmless to fish, plants, and mammals.
    • Insect Growth Regulators (Pyriproxyfen): Hormonal juvenile mimics that prevent larvae from shedding their outer skin to become flying adults, breaking the reproductive chain for weeks.
    • Temephos 50% EC (Abate): Proven organophosphate larvicide for non-potable drains, stagnant swamps, and sewage channels.
  3. Continuous Monitoring & Source Reduction: We schedule bi-weekly or monthly inspections during high-risk monsoon and post-monsoon months, issuing physical compliance reports for facility managers and municipal audits.

Safety for Environment & Non-Target Organisms

Our biological larvicides selectively target the digestive alkaline gut of mosquito larvae. They do not harm ornamental pond fish (like Guppies or Koi), birds, domestic pets, or garden vegetation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Anti-Larval Treatment FAQs

Answers to key questions regarding larval management in residential and commercial premises.

Can larvicides be used in drinking water sumps or overhead tanks?

For potable drinking water storage, we strictly use mechanical inspection, non-chemical deep cleaning, and mesh installation on overflow pipes. Bio-larvicides and IGRs are reserved for non-potable rainwater harvesting pits, drainage collection sumps, elevator shafts, and wastewater tanks.

How quickly does the anti-larval treatment kill wrigglers?

BTI bio-larvicides kill active larvae within 12 to 24 hours of ingestion. IGR formulations prevent pupae from emerging into adults, preventing newly spawned generations from taking flight over an extended 21-to-30 day window.

Why is fogging alone not enough to solve a mosquito issue?

Thermal fogging only kills mosquitoes that are currently flying through the smoke. If nearby stagnant drains or basements contain millions of larvae, new adult swarms will emerge within 48 to 72 hours. Anti-larval treatment attacks the biological root of the infestation.

How often should anti-larval treatment be conducted?

During the monsoon and humid post-monsoon months (June through November), anti-larval treatments are most effective when refreshed every 14 to 21 days to cover the mosquito incubation cycle.
