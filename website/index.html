Pest control in Hyderabad

Pest control services in Hyderabad, priced before we visit

Green Pest Management Solution is a Hyderabad pest control company based in Hafeezpet. We treat apartments, independent houses, villas, offices, shops and restaurants for termites, bed bugs, cockroaches, rats and mice, mosquitoes and mosquito larvae, across the city from Kondapur and HITEC City to Secunderabad and Uppal.

Published prices, not surprise quotes

Most pest control services in Hyderabad quote only after an inspection. We publish our prices by home size: cockroach treatment for a 1 BHK is ₹1,300 and anti-termite treatment starts at ₹3,599. We confirm the final price on WhatsApp before anyone comes, and you pay only after the job is done.

Treatment matched to Hyderabad's seasons

The monsoon months bring termite swarms and mosquito breeding in sumps, drains and open plots, while the dry months push cockroaches towards kitchens and bathrooms. We plan each treatment around the pest, the season and the building, whether that is a gated-community flat in Kondapur or an independent house in Miyapur.

Safe for families, backed by a written warranty

We use insecticides registered for household pest control, applied at label doses, and tell you in advance which rooms to avoid and for how long. Termite treatment comes with a 5-year warranty, bed bug and cockroach treatment with 6 months, and every warranty includes free revisits.

Pest control questions, answered

Straight answers about cost, safety and booking. Still unsure? Call 094916 56200.

How much does pest control cost in Hyderabad?

Our published prices start at ₹1,300 for cockroach treatment in a 1 BHK, ₹1,699 for bed bug treatment and ₹3,599 for anti-termite treatment. Larger homes cost more; see the full price list. Rodent and mosquito treatments are priced after a quick look at photos. We confirm the final price on WhatsApp before anyone visits.

Which areas of Hyderabad do you cover?

All of Hyderabad. We are based in Hafeezpet and regularly treat homes in Kondapur, HITEC City, Miyapur, Kukatpally, KPHB Colony, Nallagandla, Tellapur, Manikonda, Tolichowki, Secunderabad, Uppal, Tarnaka and Banjara Hills. See all service areas.

Is pest control safe for children and pets?

Yes, when the after-care steps are followed. We use insecticides registered for household use in India, applied at the dose on the label, and tell you before we start which rooms to keep children and pets out of and for how long.

Do I need to leave my home during the treatment?

Not for cockroach gel treatment. For bed bug and termite treatments we tell you in advance how long to stay out of the treated rooms, usually a few hours.

Do you give a warranty?

Yes. Termite treatment carries a 5-year warranty, and bed bug and cockroach treatments carry 6 months. If the pest comes back within that period, the revisit is free. Details are on the guarantee page.

How do I book a pest control visit?

Call or WhatsApp 094916 56200 (Monday to Saturday, 8 AM to 8 PM) with your area and home size. We confirm the price and a time slot, and you pay after the treatment is done.

Do you treat offices, shops and restaurants?

Yes. Commercial premises are quoted after a few photos or a short site visit, so the treatment plan matches the size of the space and the type of pest.
